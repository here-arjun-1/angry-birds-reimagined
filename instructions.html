ANGRY BIRDS

HOW TO PLAY

Learn the controls and rules to destroy the pigs and complete the level.

CONTROLS

Aim: Drag the bird to adjust the direction and power.

Launch: Release the mouse button to launch the bird.

Special Ability: Click while the bird is flying to activate its ability.

OBJECTIVE

Goal: Destroy all the pigs to complete the level.

Destroy: Break wooden, glass, and other structures.

Birds: Each bird can be used once.

SCORING SYSTEM

Score: Destroy objects and pigs to earn points.
