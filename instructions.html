HOW TO PLAY

🎯 CONTROLS

🖱️ Drag the Bird - Pull the bird back using your mouse.

🚀 Release - Release the mouse to launch the bird.

🎯 Aim - Use the slingshot to aim at the structures and pigs.

⚡ Special Ability - Click while the bird is flying to activate its special ability.

📜 RULES

🐦 Use the birds to destroy the pigs and their structures.

🏗️ Destroying more objects gives you a higher score.

🎯 Try to use as few birds as possible.

❤️ You lose a bird if it misses the target or leaves the screen.

🏆 Clear all the pigs to complete the level.
